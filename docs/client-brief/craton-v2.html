01 /The Craton mindset

The next breakthrough starts with a better question.

What if complex information could become clearer decisions? We bring bold thinking, deep research, and thoughtful architecture together to build products for work where trust is the hard part.

RAccelerator · In development

Regulatory clarity, built around evidence.

RAccelerator is an AI-enabled SaaS platform for EU MDR and IVDR regulatory affairs. It connects requirements, technical documentation, and supporting evidence in a workflow that regulatory experts review — with the reasoning traceable on every output.

Device classification
MDR and IVDR classification with traceable rule reasoning, drawn from the device’s own documentation and only the questions that matter.
GSPR gap assessment
Evidence mapped to each Annex I requirement, grouped by chapter, with critical gaps and evidence documents visible at a glance.
Remediation
MDD-to-MDR and IVDD-to-IVDR remediation planned next, followed by regulatory intelligence and lifecycle management.
Human oversight
Expert review and visible exceptions stay central to the workflow. RAccelerator supports regulatory judgment; it does not replace it.

Development-stage product. Illustrations on this site are conceptual, not screenshots of a production system. They do not represent regulatory approval, guaranteed compliance, or verified performance results.

ReviewsIntel · Patent pending

The evidence layer for agentic commerce.

ReviewsIntel connects product-review evidence with agentic purchase decisions, so the relationship between a recommendation, its context, and its supporting evidence is clear before anything is bought.

Review evidence
Relevant review information enters the decision as evidence, not as disconnected text.
Decision context
Evidence is related to the actual decision and the needs it is meant to serve.
Evidence-backed authorization
A purchase is authorized on what the evidence supports, with the rationale visible to the buyer.
Built in the open
Developed with an AI-native, role-based software lifecycle — the same discipline behind every Craton product.
